Scope and caveats

Current boundaries of the implementation

Known representation artifact

State-file axis coefficients are constant-extrapolated from instead of stored as zero. They do not enter forces; real axis geometry agrees.

gauge

Multigrid and single-grid runs may converge to different members of a near-degenerate -gauge family while representing the same physical equilibrium.

Free-boundary qualification

Double axisymmetric Solovev is qualified. General -D and float free-boundary validation are still pending.

Out of scope

Mercier stability, jxbout, wout, and a Python interface are not implemented; they are post-processing or interface work outside the core loop.

Core fixed-boundary solver is design-completePedagogical architecture, not production certification
